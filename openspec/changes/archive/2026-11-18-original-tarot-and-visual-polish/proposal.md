# Add original tarot content and visual polish

Replace the tarot placeholders with original Oracle card interpretations and artwork, record content provenance, and improve the card presentation and responsive conversation navigation.

## Why

The approved delivery plan reserves original content and visual refinement for the later polish milestone. The functional app and fixed draw are now in place, so the card catalog and visual system can be completed without changing the reading boundary.

## Scope

- Add upright and reversed interpretations for every card in the 78-card deck.
- Record interpretation, naming, and illustration provenance.
- Add original vector Major Arcana scenes and suit-specific Minor Arcana art.
- Improve card clarity, reversed orientation, focus states, and mobile conversation navigation.

## Non-goals

- Celtic Cross, generated raster art, additional divination systems, hosted model use, or web search.
