## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Tellers interpret application-provided draws

Tarot tellers SHALL interpret the shared spread and their own signature card as supplied by the application. They SHALL NOT claim to perform an independent draw, change the spread or their signature card, or claim to search for current information.

#### Scenario: Teller receives its signature card
- **WHEN** a teller generates its reading
- **THEN** its input contains the shared spread and only its own signature card
